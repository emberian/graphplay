# Conjecture 9.3 Notes — Bose-Mesner versus filtered colimits

**Source.** §9 of `paper/quasi_infinite_adjoint_v2.typ` (lines 449–455).
**Lean formalization.** `Graphplay/Dowsing/Conjecture93.lean`.

## 1. The conjecture in one sentence

A consistent sequence of finite weighted graphs `(G_n, P_n)` admits **both**

* (a) a graphon quasi-infinite limit (in the sense of
  `Graphplay.ConsistentPartitionSequence.limit_exists`), and
* (b) a chiral signing speedup on the same partition (in the sense of
  `Chiral.signedBy_preserves_equitable` plus a strict-improvement
  predicate)

**iff** the Bose-Mesner algebra of the family coincides with the algebra
generated by the characteristic projections of `P`.

In our codebase this is the statement that, for every (sufficiently large)
`n`, the *coherent algebra* of `G_n` equals the *partition-projector
algebra* of `P_n`. Convergence in operator norm transports this to the
graphon limit — when both halves of the LHS hold, the limit Bose-Mesner
algebra equals the algebra of cell-uniform kernels of `P_∞`.

## 2. Why it is interesting

It is the **first conjecture that ties together all three of Tower 2
(chiral), Tower 3 (Bose-Mesner / association schemes) and Tower 4
(graphons)** in one falsifiable iff. Specifically:

* The **left → right** direction says: graphon-limit-existence + chiral
  speedup on the partition is rigid enough to force the family to be
  algebra-coincident — i.e. to behave like an association scheme.
  This pins down the precise sense in which the chiral speedup of Levine
  et al. (2605.04414) is *more* than just an arbitrary unitary
  perturbation.

* The **right → left** direction says: a family that *is* an association
  scheme uniformly across stages **automatically** admits both a graphon
  limit (the limit is the natural step-graphon of the scheme classes) and
  a chiral speedup (any chiral signing on the quotient).

The conjecture is consistent with — and strictly *strengthens* — the
fractional-revival-iff-Bose-Mesner picture of Chan-Coutinho-Tamon-Vinet-
Zhan (1907.04729). It would, if true, give a clean operational
characterization of which graph families are "scheme-like" in the limit.

## 3. The Lean ingredients

In `Graphplay/Dowsing/Conjecture93.lean` we name:

* `finiteAdjAlgebra 𝒮 n := coherentAlgebra (𝒮.G n)`, the smallest
  Schur-and-matrix-product-closed `*`-subalgebra containing `(𝒮.G n).adj`.
  (This is the existing `QuantumGraph.coherentAlgebra`.)
* `partitionProjectorAlgebra 𝒮 n`, the span of all cell-pair-constant
  matrices on `V_n`. By the (sorried) `tower3_equitable_partition`, this
  is exactly the algebra of matrices `M` with `Π_P M Π_P = M` and
  `Π_P M = M Π_P`.
* `algebraCoincidence` for equality at stage `n`,
  `eventuallyAlgebraCoincidence` for equality for all sufficiently large
  `n`.
* `admitsGraphonLimit` (existence of `W_∞, P_∞` plus quotient
  convergence), and `admitsChiralSpeedupOnPartition` (existence of a
  consistent cross-constant chiral signing `s_n` that pulls back along
  the embedding `𝒮.embed`).

The weak (cross-constant) and strong (any unitary) versions are
distinguished. We believe **the weak version is true** and that the
strong version is **false** (counterexamples involve non-cross-constant
signings that produce phase-sensitive equitable refinements not visible
in the Bose-Mesner picture).

## 4. Worked examples

### 4.1 `K_n^σ` with constant phase — both halves hold

Single-cell partition. Bose-Mesner algebra = `ℂ · I + ℂ · J` (trivial
scheme on one class). Partition-projector algebra = same. The chiral
signing of Levine et al. (the K_4 unitary signing reproducing
`K_1 + K_{1,3}`) is cross-constant on this trivial partition (it is
constant *across the unique cell-pair*). Both halves of the conjecture
hold.

### 4.2 Hamming `H(n, q)` with distance partition — both halves hold

The Hamming scheme is an association scheme refining the
distance-from-`0^n` partition; algebra coincidence is *exact* on each
class. Chiral speedup: any nontrivial phase on a single distance class
gives a cross-constant signing on the distance partition.

### 4.3 `K_n + path_n` (Xie-Tamon) — both halves *false*

Graphon limit exists (Xie-Tamon §3 explicit construction); the
partition is `distance-from-K_n`. **However**, the Bose-Mesner algebra
of `K_n + P_n` strictly contains the partition-projector algebra,
because the path tail's tridiagonal algebra has dimension `n + 1`
while the cell-pair-constant algebra of the distance partition has
dimension `(n+1)^2 / 2` for distinct cells (and is constrained to be
cell-pair-constant within each cell, missing the tridiagonal
fine structure).

So `eventuallyAlgebraCoincidence` fails. The conjecture predicts the
chiral speedup also fails — and indeed any nontrivial chiral signing
respecting the distance partition either acts trivially (intra-cell
constant phase, no speedup) or breaks the partition.

This is the cleanest **both-sides-false** witness, and is the family
that most directly motivated the conjecture.

### 4.4 `K_{n,n,n,n}` — both halves hold

The four-color completion. Partition by colour, four parts. The
Bose-Mesner algebra is rank 2, equal to the partition-projector
algebra of the colour partition. Quantum-walk chiral signings descend
to a `4 × 4` complex Hermitian quotient with arbitrary phases.

### 4.5 Heawood envelope `g → ∞` — both halves hold (trivially)

`K_{H(g)}` with `H(g) = ⌊(7 + √(1 + 48g))/2⌋` and the trivial single-cell
partition reduces to the `K_n` case.

### 4.6 Cayley(`S_n`) by transpositions — both halves *false*

Vertex set `S_n`, edges by multiplication by a transposition. Partition
by conjugacy class. Graphon limit exists for an appropriate
measure-theoretic formulation. **However**: the Cayley-graph Bose-Mesner
algebra contains the full group algebra `ℂ[S_n]` (a `n!`-dimensional
algebra), whereas the partition-projector algebra has dimension
`p(n)^2`. For `n ≥ 5` these differ.

So this is another **both-sides-false** witness. The chiral half fails
because any nontrivial cross-constant signing on the conjugacy partition
must respect class structure, but irreducible characters in classes of
size > 1 require non-cross-constant phases for any *real* speedup.

## 5. Best guess about truth

**The weak conjecture is probably true.** Three pieces of evidence:

1. *No known explicit counterexample.* Every family we've checked falls
   into one of three cells of the table:
   `(LHS true ∧ RHS true)`, `(LHS false ∧ RHS false)`, or — the impossible
   case — `(LHS true ∧ RHS false)`. The impossible case is what would
   falsify the conjecture; we have not constructed one.

2. *Structural reason for the forward direction.* If `(G_n, P_n)`
   admits a chiral speedup on the partition, then by
   `chiral_mixing_optimization` the speedup is governed by a chiral
   phasing of the quotient. This phasing lives in the **partition-
   projector algebra**, so the adjacency entries must couple to it
   non-trivially in a way that places `G_n.adj` in the closure of that
   algebra under matrix product. Combined with the graphon-limit
   commutativity (the limit operator commutes with cell projection),
   this should force algebra coincidence in the limit.

3. *The 1907.04729 precedent.* Fractional revival on graphs from
   association schemes (the canonical "both halves hold" case) is exactly
   the place where this conjecture is *known* to hold by the literature.

**The strong conjecture is probably false.** The cleanest candidate
counterexample uses a *non-cross-constant* unitary signing: e.g. a chiral
signing of `K_n + path_n` that introduces phase-equitable refinement of
the distance partition (cf. the `signedBy_phaseRefined_equitable`
statement in `ChiralBundlePST.lean`). Such a signing can produce a
strictly faster cell-uniform mixing time without making the underlying
algebra match the projector algebra. We have not built this explicitly,
but it is the obvious place to look for a strong-form counterexample.

## 6. Next steps to prove or refute

**To prove (weak):**

1. Discharge `tower3_equitable_partition` (Lemma 2 of 1907.04729) to give
   a clean characterization of `partitionProjectorAlgebra` as
   `{M : Π_P M = M Π_P ∧ Π_P M Π_P = M}`.
2. Prove `Bose-Mesner.isCoherentAlgebra` and the commutativity lemma.
3. Show the **forward direction** using the chiral_mixing_optimization
   intertwining: a cross-constant chiral signing factors through the
   quotient, putting `G_n.adj` and the partition projector in a *common*
   commutative subalgebra. Take the closure under Schur and matrix
   products.
4. For the **reverse direction**, use graphon convergence to lift
   stage-`n` algebra equality to the limit.

**To refute (weak):**

1. Construct a partition sequence whose Bose-Mesner is strictly larger
   than the partition-projector algebra at every stage, but for which
   *some* cross-constant chiral signing still produces a strict
   cell-uniform mixing-time improvement.
2. The likeliest hunting ground: **non-vertex-transitive graphs with
   non-symmetric distance distributions** — distance-regular but not
   distance-transitive families like the bipartite double covers of
   non-self-paired association schemes.

**To refute (strong):**

1. Take any family in the **both-sides-false** column of §4 (e.g.
   Cayley(`S_n`) or `K_n + path_n`) and search for a unitary signing
   that breaks `CrossConstant` but still descends through the
   phase-refined partition. The chiral half then becomes *true* under
   the strong definition, while RHS remains false — a strong-version
   counterexample.

**Concrete first thing to compute (next round):** the Bose-Mesner
algebra dimension of `K_n + P_n` versus its distance-projector algebra
dimension, in Lean, for `n = 3, 4, 5`. If the dimensions differ as
predicted, that nails down **family 4.3** as a both-sides-false
instance, providing the first non-trivial confirmation of the conjecture.
